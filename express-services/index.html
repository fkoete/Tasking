Transcription & Handwriting Digitisation

Accurate transcription packages for recordings and verified digitisation of handwritten pages.

The transcription minimum charge is R120 per project, regardless of audio length. Above the minimum, pricing increases by audio minute according to audio quality, complexity and turnaround. A final quote confirms the applicable tier.

View transcription tiers, add-ons and board support
Transcription rates per audio minute
  • AI-assisted + human edit - R8-R15: clear audio, single speaker and standard turnaround.
  • Standard human transcription - R15-R25: multi-speaker, professional quality, typical 2-3 day turnaround. Median professional rate: R22.50 per audio minute.
  • High-accuracy / specialised - R25-R40+: legal, board, technical, poor audio or urgent turnaround.
Handwriting digitisation (OCR + verification)

OCR processing followed by line-by-line human verification against the original image. Clean/printed material: R250-R350 per hour or R15-R25 per page. Minimum charge: one hour or five pages, whichever is greater. Cursive or poor handwriting may attract a premium.

Hard-copy add-ons, itemised
  • Black-and-white, double-sided printing: R2-R4 per page
  • Staple or comb binding: R5-R15
  • Document pack or envelope: R11.50
  • Counter-to-Door courier, up to 1 kg: R85.80-R94.39
  • PostNet2PostNet courier, up to 5 kg: R109

Printing, binding, packaging and courier are quoted as separate line items and are not included in the transcription or per-page digitisation rate.

Board Secretariat Support

Quoted as a project fee or monthly retainer, based on meetings per year, board size, virtual or in-person attendance, action-register maintenance and turnaround. Transcription of recorded meetings is priced separately.

Deliverables are packaged according to the selected transcription type and agreed requirements. File format, turnaround and scope are confirmed in the quote.
